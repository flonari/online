Rules

Move one non-exhausted wizard to an empty tile touching (including diagonally) another of your wizards. You can't move to a tile with 3 or more enemy wizards around it. A moved wizard is exhausted (shown turned sideways); when all yours are exhausted they refresh. A wizard with 3 or more enemies around it is captured. With fewer than 5 wizards on the board, spend your turn placing a reserve wizard on an empty tile of your home row (it arrives exhausted). Capture 3 enemy wizards to win.
