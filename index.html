Rules

Move one non-exhausted wizard to an empty tile touching (including diagonally) another of your wizards. You can't move to a tile with 3 or more enemy wizards around it. A moved wizard is exhausted (shown turned sideways); when all yours are exhausted they refresh. A wizard with 3 or more enemies around it is captured. With fewer than 5 wizards on the board, spend your turn placing a reserve wizard on an empty tile of your home row (it arrives exhausted). Reduce your opponent to 2 wizards on the board to win.

Rules

Each side has 3 guards and 1 Wise Elder, starting as shown on the board. On your turn move 2 different units (the very first move of the game is only 1 unit). A unit slides any number of tiles in a straight line, orthogonal or diagonal, over empty tiles only. When a unit stops next to enemy units (any of the 8 tiles around it), each of them is pushed 1 tile directly away, if that tile is empty. At the end of your turn all your units must form one orthogonally connected cluster, or you lose. A unit with no orthogonal friend at the start of your turn is lonesome and can't move that turn, so rescue it. If your Wise Elder stands on the far edge at the end of your turn and your units are connected, you win. If you can't take a full turn that ends connected, you lose.

Rules

Each side has 4 Messengers and 1 Stone Cylinder, set up as shown on the board. The first color starts. On your turn move one of your Messengers 1 or 2 steps in a straight line in any of the 8 directions, over empty tiles only. It must then Throw: pick a unit on one of the 8 tiles around it, and that unit lands on the tile on the exact opposite side of the Messenger. That tile must be on the board and empty. You can throw your own Stone Cylinder and any Messenger, but never the enemy Stone Cylinder. An enemy Messenger you throw is stunned: it can't move on its owner's next turn, but it can still be thrown. After a throw, if other non-stunned Messengers of yours stand next to the landing tile, you may throw that unit again with one of them, once per Messenger. A Messenger that can't move to a tile where it can throw can't be moved. Get your Stone Cylinder onto the row at the far end to win at once. If you have no legal move you pass.
